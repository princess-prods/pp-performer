# Branding & Copy Guidelines

## Core Principles

The recruitment experience must be **professional, transparent, and empowering**. Users should feel they are exploring a legitimate opportunity, not being pressured or exploited.

### Key Values
1. **Self-selection over targeting** - Let interested adults come to us
2. **Transparency** - Clear about what we offer and what participation involves
3. **No obligation** - Inquiry is not commitment
4. **Professionalism** - Present as a legitimate creator/modeling opportunity
5. **Respect for autonomy** - "You decide what opportunities you're interested in"

## Tone of Voice

### DO
- Emphasize the opportunity, not financial need
- Use professional, clear language
- Make verification and requirements feel protective, not invasive
- Communicate that participation is voluntary at every stage
- Welcome both new and experienced performers equally

### DO NOT
- Use urgency or pressure tactics ("Make money fast!", "Need cash?")
- Reference financial hardship or vulnerability
- Use explicit descriptions of sexual acts in recruitment copy
- Make promises about earnings
- Use language that could feel coercive

## Example Copy

### Welcome/Hero
> "Interested in Performing?"
>
> We're currently accepting inquiries from adults 18+ interested in paid adult-content production.

### Value Proposition
- Paid production opportunities
- Professional, private production environment
- Flexible scheduling
- New & experienced performers welcome
- Clear compensation & agreements
- Content-sharing/licensing opportunities may be available

### Before Form Submission
> "Before contacting us, please know:
> - You must be 18 or older
> - Government-issued identification is required
> - Compensation is discussed before any production
> - Participation is voluntary
> - You can ask questions before deciding whether to participate
> - You'll receive information about how the finished content will be used"

### After Submission
> "Submitting this form does not commit you to participating in a production. It is an inquiry only. Any production opportunity, compensation, content rights, and participation terms will be discussed and agreed upon separately."

### Age Gate
> "Adult Performer Opportunities"
>
> "You must be 18 years of age or older to continue."

## What NOT to Include

Never use copy that:
- Mentions "single moms," "college girls," "women in recovery," etc.
- Implies financial desperation is a qualification
- Uses explicit imagery or descriptions
- Promises specific earnings
- Creates artificial urgency

## Legal Requirements

All recruitment messaging must include:
- 18+ age requirement
- Government ID verification required
- Clear statement that inquiry is not commitment
